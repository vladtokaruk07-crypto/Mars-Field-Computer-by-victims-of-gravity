Mission parameters

Model assumptions
  • Time is Martian: 1 sol = 24 Martian hours = 24 h 39 min 35 s on an Earth clock, so a Martian hour ≈ 61.6 min. All rates below are per sol and per Mars hour.
  • Oxygen in the module: ≈ 24.7 L per Mars hour per person (≈ 0.85 kg/sol); during an EVA ≈ 46.2 L per Mars hour. +30% reserve.
  • Water: 3 L for drinking and food and 1 L for hygiene per person per sol, +0.5 L per Mars hour for suit cooling. +20% reserve.
  • One suit battery charge ≈ 8 Mars hours of EVA. Rough terrain and cold increase consumption.
  • Radiation: dose per sol × number of sols. Reference measured in Gale Crater: 0.67 mSv per Earth day ≈ 0.69 mSv/sol. For other sites it is an estimate.
  • This is an educational model for a hackathon, not instructions for a real flight.
